## Y1 • Week 51
This week two years ago I was cutting through an overgrown garden – nervously waiting a visa (staying with family and overanxious). Today I was wrangling a chainsaw, cutting through overgrown vine on a side wall… until my neighbour suggested it was too hot for working and I should go to the sea for a swim. My current resident permit is being processed (runs out in 10 days) and I’m not anxious at all 🐼.

Yearly vehicle registration is not a thing in France. If you buy a new car, after four years it needs to be assessed regularly. Purchase details and numberplate stay with the car forever.

Went with a group for dinner at a crazy looking beach shack – great views and nice food. I remember before expo ’88 in Brisbane we didn’t have footpath dining when the rest of the world did. Sometimes I do have a rumbly tummy after eating out… not this time 🤩
