I’m on a roll now that I have my residence permit renewal. This week I sent off an application for a social security number… which precedes a health care card, a tax return and a French licence. Some people I know are moving back to the UK and I mentioned that I’d appreciate anything they didn’t sell at their garage sale. I now have a library to dream for… making me look way smarter than I am 😁. Shelving happening once floor and walls done!

Work continues at home although it’s been a slow couple of weeks. Tiling is hard and it’s still warm when up and down a ladder. Hopefully grouting this week and will send a photo of me in the shower when finally finished (NOT).
