## Y2 • Week 11
Celebrating Halloween is big here – pumpkins and scary shit for sale everywhere (although not in my Muslim neighbourhood😊). France and the US have a relationship built on shared history,  so we follow the tradition popularised by the US. A few of us were going to wander into town this evening to be part of the show… but have decided to stay home due to continuing riots 😯.

I’ve removed three shrubs in the front yard which means I can begin levelling to make ready for the pond. In amongst fireplace, shower and pond it’s been a slow and steady week. A group of us headed to the local markets (4 min walk from my place) earlier today and stocked up on spices and sweets – I’m currently cooking more than eating so have a freezer full of fine food 🤩.

Daylight savings kicked in this week, and my body programmed for a 7am start now wakes me up at 6am and I’m loving the extra hour. On the day it changed I made the mistake of turning up at someone’s front door for coffee an hour early… luckily I wasn’t the first to arrive!
