## Y2 • Week 5
Received the following yesterday… “I am writing to inform you that your application for a residence permit has been accepted. A new residence permit is being processed and will be issued to you shortly.” Someone is pedalling hard at the Ministry for Foreign Affairs as the first visa took 11 months to process – this one just one month. Will again be for one year which I’m prepared for this time 😁.

This weekend we’re celebrating European Heritage Day allowing free entry to theatres, galleries, schools and churches… some that are usually not open to the public. A few theatres and a hotel have been restored to their Belle Epoque era (1871-1914) beauty.

Since last weekend, while still in bed I hear distant popping sounds… picked up my phone to discover shooting season started Sun 13th and runs through to February. Some things you are just better off not knowing 😯. And totally unrelated apart from the fact I was still holding my mobile... I was scrolling through a French lesson that asked how would you translate “I have missed my train”? Some smart person replied “Merde!” (Shit!).
