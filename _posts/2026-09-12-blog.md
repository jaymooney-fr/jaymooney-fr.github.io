## Y2 • Week 4
Even the smallest towns in France have clubs that promote themselves on Association Day. I wandered through the city centre (over 100 stalls) and picked up a few brochures. I’ll join a group or two for the chatter – that don’t involve knitting 🤣. It was nice running into members of the photography club and choir who remembered me… clubs that I am no longer part of.

I’m slowly learning more of the revered history of Saint Aphrodisius – the first bishop and patron saint of Béziers. He was an Egyptian priest arriving on his camel in the third century. Beheaded by the Romans in a square in Béziers, he is said to have picked up his head, carrying it under his arm to his living space which is now Saint-Aphrodise Basilica. Camels remain cherished here and of course we hold an annual festival to celebrate (with a fake one). The Basilica has had many uses over the centuries, now showing art and relics (real human bones) – a beautiful church to visit.

Wall tiling continues – I’m still dropping much glue on the floor and spacing is shit – hoping grout hides some of the variance. I’m learning heaps and confident that by the time I start upstairs I’ll know what I’m doing!!
