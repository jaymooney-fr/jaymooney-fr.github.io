## Y2 • Week 7
It’s raining while I’m writing this… we’ve been in drought for so long that a total water ban is in place including watering gardens and topping up pools. Washing cars is okay at stations and cheap at 6€ • nobody is doing this as cars are again dusty the next day.

Only because it’s funny… After being notified our resident permits are approved, we wait a week to receive an SMS saying they’re ready for collection. I immediately sign in to make an appointment to collect, however it doesn’t recognise the SMS has been sent. Next day I try again and successfully make an appointment… accidently not in Béziers. Cancel the appointment immediately and try again… the cancellation is not recognised so I’m unable to make a new appointment. Few hours later I successfully make an appointment for later this month… and life goes on 🤣

And because it’s even funnier… went to pay my rates online. I needed to give my bank details, and permission for the government to withdraw the amount from my account on Oct 1. My right eye is still twitching. Just now I thought I should check that it’s been debited – it has 😀.
