## Y2 • Week 9
France has a strategy to combat the importation of palm oil by 2030. It’s nice checking a processed food’s ingredients to find no palm oil. The availability of frozen processed food is high, as it is almost everywhere – just hopping downstairs to pop a frozen pizza into the oven 🤣

Big thing in France are antique market fairs. Visited one in Pézenas last weekend (20 minutes north) – closing the whole business district and attracting over 2000 visitors – it’s a major event. I wanted to buy something but had a 10€ limit for purchases. Ended up with a quiche and espresso… everything else was way above budget.

The mayor of Béziers is making headlines by standing up to crime. During recent student riots, he walked through a crowd and addressed the front line who were throwing molotov cocktails at a police block. He’s a small man so he took a few huge bodyguards with him. He is now denying a social housing application for the family of one of the young perpetrators. He’s both loved and hated 🤣
