## Y1 • Week 50
I have spent months procrastinating the wet area tiling due to rounded screws heads holding a wall plate onto plasterboard. Found a YouTube video that explained that once the anchors were clamped, you just unscrew the screws and remove. I don’t think I’ve ever been so happy 🤣  

Fires in France are now rated as stable but not under control. Smoke has blown into Béziers from the west coast 500km away, causing hazy skies, horrible air quality and a yellow moon. South of France has been in drought for months, so even a few raindrops lead to great jubilation. Possibly because in my suburb most people lay gravel not grass, I haven’t heard a lawn mower since moving in. Wouldn’t be on a Sunday anyway… forbidden!!

Came up on a site how relieved French people must have been when the century rolled over. Instead of needing to say “mille neuf cent quatre-vingt-dix-neuf” (1999) overnight it changed to “deux mille” (2000). And a fun fact not related at all, is that the collective name for Bézier locals is “Les Bitterois”. We even have our own dessert La Bitterois🤣 it is good, however I'm yet to try a pastry that I don't like!
