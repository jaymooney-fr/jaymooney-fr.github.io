## Y2 • Week 2
Some excitement this week with a tornado sweeping through Pomas (little village an hour away). Rare occurrence (so rare it was reported as the west coast, not the east coast). Villages including Béziers were hit with fierce winds, rain and hail for 10 minutes (not the tornado), and then we had sunshine – nothing new coming from Brisbane. Drought hardened trees cracked and flipped created debris and road closures which continue to be cleared. I’ve just returned from a few nights in Olonzac (50km west), needing to detour around two impassable points. Detours are a delight • tiny villages and countryside with endless vineyards which are almost ready for harvest.

Friday coffee club held a picnic on the beach this week. It was byo potluck to share – noodle salad was Michelin star worthy, Spanish tortilla was the best I’ve ever tasted, and I added local chicken pieces with enough ginger, garlic, lemon and oil to make them Portuguese. We ate like kings – not our current one who is a bit sick according to Camilla 😯.

Finally I’ve started cutting tiles for wet areas 😁. Staggered or not, starting from edges or centres, spacing and drilling holes for pipes have all added to the procrastination – thinking I’ve got it all sorted finally 🤣. An extension to my resident permit application has arrived automatically – very different to last year when I applied for extensions three times during the year.
