## Y2 • Week 10
I’ve lost the ability to transfer photos from my phone to computer with onedrive. It’s an easy fix but I realise how hard it would be to move country without computer skills. From scanning and merging .pdfs for online applications, to following instructions to cancel a service (still mastering this one). Being able to reverse park on the wrong side of the road and having more intestinal fortitude that the prophet Muhammad also helps 🤣.

Riots happening here which are being broadcast worldwide. Not affecting me, although I expect my insurances may be higher next year. It’s all really sad and I don’t understand enough to even comment • everyone else seems to know why and who is in the wrong. Two beautiful headlines I’ve come across are “Class Action” and “Revolting students” – by clever journalists!

Next week I’ll have photos of a finished studio bathroom and fireplace… they are soooo near to being finished! Overnight the temperature has dropped • I’ve started wearing bedsocks at night and three layers during the day. Not complaining – it’s just an observation. Still leaving a bedroom window open for the moment and enjoying snuggling under a warm quilt 🤩.
