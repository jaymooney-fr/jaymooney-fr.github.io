## Y2 • Week 8
Rain finished earlier this week, followed up by a few really windy days – Mary Poppins didn’t arrive but a beautiful chill did – never complaining about the cold again… sweat not running down glasses is a nice improvement and mozzies have disappeared, meaning I don’t need to whack them with whatever is handy… paint scraper, hammer, angle grinder!

I’ve downloaded a KIA app so I can always find my car – after losing it a few weeks ago. Twice I’ve been alerted on my phone that I’ve left it unlocked and once that the boot wasn’t closed properly. It took a neighbour to let me know that I’d left a window open overnight.

Always learning new things… like there are different grades of vinegar. In a shop I went to buy a bottle 12%. For a euro more I could buy 14%. I had no idea there were different grades. Big sister let me know you can buy 99% but proper protective clothing is needed 😁
