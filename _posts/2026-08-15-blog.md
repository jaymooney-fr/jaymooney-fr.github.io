## Y1 • Week 52
Béziers holds a festival every year and a group of us walked down the main street after watching the eclipse. Our 80000 population bumps up to two million making the place very busy and noisy for five nights. I’m out in the suburbs so not affected – people living in the city work around road closures and loud music.

I held my first dinner party tonight since reclaiming the lounge and dining nook. Seven guests and three courses. Cooked and cleaned all day – three men (husbands of friends) thought it was amazing that an Australian could cook as well as a French person 😁.

Incredible to think that I’ve been here two years this week. Happy to count dinner tonight as the celebration, but I’m thinking I should maybe start some tiling to mark the major milestone 🤣.
