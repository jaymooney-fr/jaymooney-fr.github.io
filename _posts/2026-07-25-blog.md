## Y1 • Week 49
In 2015 the French Scrabble championship was won by a New Zealander who didn’t speak a word of French. He memorised the entire French Scrabble dictionary in just nine weeks. He repeated his streak in 2014 by winning the Spanish Scrabble championship without speaking Spanish 🤣.

Friday night I watched a light show projected onto the outside walls of a Béziers’ church (famous for the bloodiest episode in local history in 1209). Crazy great!! I always hesitate and whinge when shows start after 10pm but this was worth the effort. Tonight I attended a jazz night in Vias, a small village 15 minutes away… also with gigs starting late. I have been sleeping in, just need to say!!

On our resident cards there are varnished notes which I recognised as “Ode to joy”. I didn’t understand why German music would be printed on French cards, until a friend let me know Beethoven’s ninth is the EU anthem. Thinking this might be useful for Trivia buffs 🤩
